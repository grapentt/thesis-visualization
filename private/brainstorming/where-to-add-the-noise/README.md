# Where to Add the Noise: visualization programme (design, 2026-11-19)

Design documents for a series of thesis videos and explorables about the riemannian-generative project.
Nothing here is built yet. Thesis pieces will live in `src/videos/<id>` and `explorables/<id>` once built.

- `programme.md`: the final programme. Pitch, spine and Musts, programme map, devices and visual language,
  scene-by-scene storyboards (P0, E1–E6, X1–X3), computation catalogue (rigs R1–R34), data assets (A1–A30),
  number canon, risks and hypotheses, build plan, cuts, change log and unresolved items.
- `understanding/`: the reading reports the design rests on (Era 1 wδ, stiffness, embeddings and ALDP5, BBA and
  learned maps, information geometry, range and order, current frontier, systems and data, origins, straightening,
  literature, number canon, existing animations) plus the completeness critic and an index of 216 visual ideas.
- `design-round/`: the brief, five independent designs (story, mechanism, evidence, interaction, visual), three
  judges (fidelity, pedagogy, feasibility), the synthesis and four verifiers (numbers, honesty, completeness,
  feasibility) whose findings `programme.md` applies.

Sources: ML repo snapshot of origin/main on 2026-11-19 (theory notes 00–30, status, lab entries to 11-18, open PRs
#1104, #1113, #1136, #1137, #1138, #1141) and this repo's main plus the open PR #3 and side-video branches.
