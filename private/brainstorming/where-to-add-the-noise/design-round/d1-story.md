## 1. Organizing idea

The programme tells the project as **one question that got sharper six times**. It began as "which geometry puts unphysical molecular shapes far away?" and is now "what must the noise keep readable, in which order, and what can that answer not supply?" Each episode poses one version of the question and shows the attempt it motivated. It shows why the attempt looked promising for a measured reason, lets the viewer believe it with a real success, and shows the measurement that broke it. It names the mechanism with its epistemic badge and ends with **question surgery**: the measured reason for leaving is rewritten, word by word, into the next and sharper question.

Three instruments recur and hold the arc together:
- the **Journey Map**: the line of questions, with verdict stamps and the threads that link a plant in one episode to its payoff in another;
- the **Erasure Clock**: one shared log-t axis on which every chart the project tried gets a lane, showing what its noise erases and when;
- the **List of Musts**: every failure leaves one design requirement, and the list grows episode by episode.

The arc closes where the instruments meet. The last lane of the Clock (ordered internal coordinates) satisfies every Must but one. The Must it fails, the excluded-volume wall, is exactly the open question. The proposed remedy is a hypothesis that rhymes with the first episode: the collision barrier of wδ.

This structure fits the audience. Supervisors and examiners judge whether the path was rational, and a question-led arc makes each reversal a result rather than an embarrassment. ML and physics peers want mechanisms, and every hinge here is a measured or derived mechanism. The project's own epistemic labels ([S]/[D]/[M]/[H]) become a visual grammar instead of footnotes.

### 2.4 Order of reveals (the dramatic shape)

- **E1** is a fall. Elegance first (barriers, cylinder), then the cost, then the wall exactly where the data live. The baseline that moved is revealed last, so the viewer learns the lesson the project learned: fix the ruler before comparing.
- **E2** is a deliberate false summit. Its climax is the promise (synchronized erasure, E4), stated with full derivational confidence and one small "?" pin.
- **E3** is a success with honest limits. The cone is a visual proof, ALDP5 results are real, and three quiet footnotes are planted.
- **E4** is the fall on 28 beads. The 2×2 of failure, then the autopsy (each failure measured), then the mechanism: the fold dissolves in the same band as the detail. The "?" pin blinks.
- **E5** is the reversal and the answer. Measurement before picture (the Clock is measured, then painted), the October goal named as the cause, then anti-order, the loud modes, the constraint split, and a prediction that came true.
- **E6** is the test and the open end. The answer holds at every budget. A one-day correction (the volume term) is shown as a correction. Then the wall, the callback hypothesis, repairs that work today, and the drift that does not stop. It ends on the Ledger.

## 4. Recurring visual devices and conventions

**Colour: one meaning per colour across the whole programme.** The house palette is extended as follows:

| Colour | Meaning in this programme |
|---|---|
| stiff orange `#ef8a5b` | local stiff features: bonds, angles, stiff modes |
| soft blue `#6cc4e8` | torsions and soft modes |
| teal `#5fd3b0` (new meaning) | collective shape: Rg, long-range distances, loud modes a₁ and a₂ ("the fold") |
| rose `#f497c0` (new meaning) | contacts, excluded volume, the collision barrier and the wall |
| lavender `#c39bf5` | readability and information: R_h curves, posterior clouds, "decided" fill (as in Retention) |
| gold `#f2cf63` | the object of attention only: playhead, current station, cursor |
| hatched ink | the planarity (rank) barrier |

Charts and models never get a colour; they are identified by lane labels and mono pictograms (Cartesian #, chain links for internal, chain plus two arrows for ordered, a log ruler for wδ). This avoids the cross-video clashes noted by a2 (rose was KL in Retention; KL is drawn lavender here).

**Feature glyphs** (used on the Clock, the rulers and every table): bond ━, angle ∠, torsion ↻, contact ⦁·⦁, long-range ↔, end-to-end ⟷, Rg ◎. Glyph fill encodes the estimator:
- filled: measured lower bound (best estimator);
- hollow: first-order screen;
- ghost outline: linear closed form;
- a right-pointing tick for "≥", a block-alone floor.

Two estimators never share a lane without this distinction (g4 §4.2).

**Journey Map.** A schematic subway line with six stations, evenly spaced, and a metric date ribbon below joined to the stations by leader lines. The ribbon is piecewise: "Jul 13 – Oct 22 · 15 weeks" | "Oct 23 – Nov 19 · 4 weeks".
- Station states: future (hollow, label "Q4" only), current (gold ring), decided (filled, with a verdict pill).
- Dead ends are short spurs ending in ⊣.
- Threads are fine coloured filaments from plant to payoff, and appear only at the payoff.
- Every episode opens (8 s) and closes (40–45 s) on it.

**Question surgery.** The old question sits under its station. Refuted words get a muted strike-through, replacement words type in gold, and the new question flies to the next station (≈ 8 s). It works like Part 1's formula surgery, applied to the research question.

**Plus/Arrow pair** (the author's own device, talk slides 3–15). A "+" line gives the measured reason it looked promising; a "→" line gives the measured reason it was left. They appear on station cards and at each attempt's verdict.

**List of Musts.** A column at the right edge of the Journey Map. Each Must has a date and the failure that produced it:
- M1 closed-form kernel;
- M2 audited baseline;
- M3 range;
- M4 noise on the molecule;
- M5 prior matching;
- M6 order;
- M7 a coordinate of its own;
- M8 exact volume term;
- M9 a wall supplied.

Ticks are ink checkmarks. The finale shows which Musts the design meets and which it meets only by repair.

**Erasure Clock.**
- Axes: log t from 10⁻⁵ to 1, 240 px per decade from x = 300; log-SNR as a secondary scale on top.
- Bands: the trained band [0.01, 1] shaded panel; the Cartesian-family band [10⁻⁵, 1] lighter.
- Lanes: 70 px each, labelled with the chart and a verdict phrase ("order without range").
- The gold playhead is the series-wide handle.

**Noise Ruler.** A compact readout cluster at the bottom right, for example "t = 0.010 · σ = 0.0446 · log-SNR 6.22 · ≙ 0.279 Å (BBA Cartesian unit 6.252 Å)". σ is always shown dimensionless first, then in the unit of the coordinates actually noised (g4 §2.1).

**Two-halves plane.** Log bond W1 against log Rg W1, with the "both right" corner shaded and floors marked. It is introduced in E4, filled in E5 and E6, and never mixes the 20,000- and 100,000-molecule panels: the panel size is printed in the corner.

**Odometer.** Paired dials, Euclidean (finite) against a chart's own distance, for approaching a degenerate configuration. Used in E1.3 and E6.5.

**Epistemic badges.** Tiny mono pills after every number:
- `M`: filled panel;
- `D`: solid outline;
- `H`: dashed outline, and the only one allowed to grow large (scene-wide in E6.5);
- `I`: dotted outline, italic;
- `S`: no outline.

A provenance stamp at (1572, 890) names the system, budget, seeds, sample count and source, for example "measured · BBA · 190,350 updates · seeds 2/3 · 100,000 molecules · #1088 (2026-11-15)".

**Correction pills.** A dated "was → now" chip (e.g. "11-15 → 11-16") wherever a reading changed. The old reading stays visible, struck through, for one beat.

**Number typography.** Plex Mono, true minus signs, the significant figures of the source, units always shown. Seeds appear as "s2 / s3". Ratios use "×" and say what is divided by what ("46–109× the floor", never "too broad"). No number is typed in a scene file: computed values come from `math.js`, and logged values from the data assets in §5.2, each with its source.

**"Decided" depiction.** In forward mode a glyph's opacity equals R_h(t). In mirror mode its lavender fill equals the decided fraction. The mirror flips the axis with a 1.2 s rotation, the y → t relabel idiom of u1-force.

### 5.3 Rule for logged numbers

A logged number (anything trained or simulated) is never computed in a scene. It is read from A9/A10/A13, shown with its badge and stamp, and pinned by a Node test against the canon value. ## 6. Risks

**Visuals that could mislead**
1. **κ arrows across definitions.** Never draw an arrow between 192 → 9.6 (residual stiffness, bgmol windows), 184 → 2.99 (1 ps step anisotropy, mdshare), 400 (toy), 2,048 (`energy.js`), ≈920 (whole data) or 1,423 (BBA mode variance). E4.2 carries an explicit caption (g4 §2.2).
2. **Mixed window estimators.** First-order (hollow) and best-estimator (filled) values never share a lane unmarked. C3's Rg window is 0.27–0.28 (canon), not 0.23, 0.19 or 0.30 (g4 §4.2). EigenFold is shown only as ratios on its own clock.
3. **Hindsight linearization.** The Journey Map orders by logic, but Q2–Q4 overlapped (notes 20–23 and the warp within 48 h; BBA from Nov 1). The date ribbon shows the overlaps, and the narration says "separated by logic, not by date". The dead-end spurs and the pre-registered misses (seed 2's 0.117; the skeleton map refuted by its own rule) stay visible, so the path does not look inevitable.
4. **The 15-week / 4-week ribbon** could read as "Era 1 was wasted". The narration states what Era 1 built: contracts, validation identities, the bug lessons, and the reframing.
5. **The mirror device** (forward played backward) is exact only for the exact reverse process. Label it, and use trained reverse-path data (#1133) only in E5.9.
6. **The barrier callback (E6.5)** is a hypothesis built on a first-order wδ window screen made by a reader (g2), not a project measurement. The barrier lives partly in the decoder. The large H badge and the stated test are mandatory. Do not imply wδ force fields are good (17–18° angle errors [M]).
7. **"Beats ScoreMD"** means beating the project's re-run of the unregularised Diffusion arm at 10% of the budget, read at 0.01/10⁻³. It is not the paper's FP model. The paper-metric PMF is count-dependent (0.080 floor at 10⁵ against the paper's 0.034 at 1.3M); never place them on one axis without the floor curve.
8. **One model, two uses (E3.5)** is "a parallel, not a head-to-head" (#768): non-conservative MLPs, Brownian dynamics, not equilibrated.

**Numbers that need care**
9. **σ units.** σ(0.01) = 0.0446 is dimensionless. It is 0.0446 Å on the manifold route, 0.042 Å as the pilots' production floor, and 0.279 Å in BBA Cartesian units. The Noise Ruler always states which.
10. **αL.** Never show note 25's 0.79–1.27 "Cramér–Rao". Use "37 visits, ~100–140 effectively independent samples, ±50% (95% CI 0.4–7.0% around 3.2%)".
11. **Bond error ratios.** Sprint 9's "50–100× too broad" is a W1-to-floor ratio: write "46–109× the floor". Sprint 7's αL 25.2% is reflection-confounded: carry the caveat.
12. **Sprint 5's JS** 0.100/0.119 uses valid samples only (77% valid). Show the validity next to it.
13. **BBA spectrum ratio.** The canon is 1,423 (note 27 §3); the local `bba_charts.json` gives 1,428. Display the value computed from the file, with its source, and narrate "about 1,400". Ask the project which fit note 27 used.
14. **Panel sizes.** 20,000- and 100,000-molecule panels have different floors (population floor 0.037 against ~0.009). The two-halves plane prints the panel size and never mixes them.
15. **Full-schedule numbers** exist only in open PR #1104 (one seed; five models chosen after stage-1 results, an amendment). Badge them "open PR · 1 seed".
16. **Force read-out conventions** (driver J^T α s(αφ) against note 29 J^T s(φ)) differ by α² on prior-matched channels. Each force visual states one convention, and the volume-term story uses the #1100 reading, not the stale status page.
